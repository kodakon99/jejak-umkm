# Jejak app (all six owner screens)

Mode: Operate. Mobile-first web app, used on low-end Android phones, Bahasa Indonesia informal.
Audience and job: Segment A warung owners (younger, app-savvy) checking KUR readiness, reconciling their Excel against the bank, exporting a receipt pack. Hero screen: Cocokkan. Must not feel like a bank/pinjol app or like accounting software.
Constraints: every number shows its source; never imply approval; owner confirms every flag.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Verification as colour taking hold. A day the bank confirms fills solid in a natural earth tone; a day that doesn't stays as open outline. No batik pattern or motif anywhere (user instruction). Refuses the white fintech card list with green/red status chips.

OWN-WORLD: Mori cream ground, indigo (nila) shell header and primary buttons, flat soga-brown fills for verified states (no texture, no pattern), red outline for selisih, amber dashed outline for cek, faint outline for menunggu. Figtree workhorse type, tabular figures on every rupiah, one leading number per screen. Rounded 6–10px, no shadows beyond one soft card shadow, no glass, no gradients.

STORY: The owner sees at a glance how much of their month the bank backs, taps a red day, sees exactly which Excel cell and statement line disagree, and fixes or explains it. They leave trusting their pack.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Indigo header (Jejak, business name, step tabs; current tab cut into the cream). Lead number "27/31 hari cocok dengan bank" at 44px soga. One line of context. 7-column month grid of square day cells. Selected red day's detail card below with source chips and two buttons: Betulkan (indigo), Jelaskan (cream).

FORM: Sogan, candidate 7 of my ordered list (re-roll 1), seed:610d8e79b75126c3. User steer: no batik design at all; keep only the natural-dye colours as flat fills. Raises: flagged day stays lit until resolved (boarding pass); status only by fill/outline (star atlas); every month same scale (botanical); one number leads each screen (Saville); plan shows only the next decision with its date at scale (terminal yellow).
